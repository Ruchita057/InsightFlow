Everything you need to analyze data

A complete analytics toolkit that runs entirely in your browser — no backend, no signup, no waiting.

CSV Upload & Demo Data

Drop any CSV or pick from built-in datasets — Retail Sales, HR Analytics, and Netflix Analytics — ready to explore instantly.

📈

Interactive Charts

Beautiful line, bar, and donut charts powered by Chart.js. Hover for details, resize responsively, and explore trends visually.

🎯

Smart KPI Cards

Automatic detection of revenue, profit, orders, customers, and more. Key metrics surface the moment your data loads.

🔍

Instantly see missing values, duplicates, numeric vs categorical columns, and completeness scores for every dataset.

Rule-Based Insights

Smart insight cards highlight top categories, growth trends, anomalies, and correlations using transparent rule-based logic.

How it works

From raw CSV to actionable insights in three steps.

1

Upload or Select

Upload your own CSV or choose a demo dataset. Data is parsed locally with Papa Parse — nothing leaves your browser.

2

Explore & Analyze

KPIs, charts, quality metrics, and smart insights appear automatically. Search, filter, and sort the full data table.

3

Decide & Share

Export a PDF report or keep exploring. Recent uploads are saved in localStorage for quick revisit.
